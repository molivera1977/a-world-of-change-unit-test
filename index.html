📋 How the Test Works

👤 Step 1 — Sign In

Choose your name. Then type your student number and tap Sign In.

📋 Step 2 — Do All Four Parts

The test has four parts: Vocabulary, Comprehension, Written Response, and Cloze. You do them in order. Each part opens when you finish the one before it. You get one try.

⚠️ No Right or Wrong Until the End

The test will not tell you if an answer is right or wrong. Read each question carefully. Pick your best answer. You will see your score at the end of each part.

📖 The Story Is Right There

In Comprehension and Written Response, the story pages are on the left side. Each question tells you which page to look at, and the story opens to that page. Look back at the story to find your answer!

✍️ Written Response

Write each answer in complete sentences, with at least 10 words. Stuck? Tap the hint button for a sentence starter. Fill in every blank before you turn it in.

📖 Reading Timer

When a question appears, the answers are locked for 12 seconds. Use that time to read the question and all the answers.

⏱️ 12 second read lock

⏱ Overall Timer

A timer runs the whole time. It is not a countdown. There is no time limit. It only shows your teacher how long you worked.
